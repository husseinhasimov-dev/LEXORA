LEXORA joins matching lemmas and parts of speech, selects senses, removes duplicates and derives cloze prompts. Original LEXORA lessons in data/lessons.tsv also use CC BY-SA 4.0. Some entries lack examples or grammar fields. FSRS scheduling uses Java-FSRS by Open Spaced Repetition under the MIT license.
https://github.com/open-spaced-repetition/java-fsrs

Privacy
Study data is stored on this device. The unconfigured build requires no account. In a configured build, signing in stores account information with Firebase and syncs packaged-word learning events to the account's Firestore collection. Device voice playback requires an installed offline German voice. Lexora does not record sound. Exported backups contain readable study data. Android's document picker controls where those files are saved.
